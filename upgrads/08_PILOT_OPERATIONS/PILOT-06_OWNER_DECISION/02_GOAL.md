# PILOT-06: Goal and Plan

## SMART Goal

> Within **2 days** of evidence completion, record an auditable owner decision with complete evidence links, conditions, and closure actions.

## Acceptance Standards

### Standard 1: Gate checklist

- [ ] Signed Charter and owner authorization.
- [ ] Daily logs complete for the observation period.
- [ ] Weekly reports reviewed.
- [ ] Usability findings dispositioned.
- [ ] Capacity thresholds reviewed.
- [ ] Security and privacy review approved.
- [ ] Open blockers have owners and dates.

### Standard 2: Decision options

- **Expand:** increase scope under new Charter.
- **Continue:** extend current observation period.
- **Remediate:** pause expansion until named issues close.
- **Stop:** disable pilot and execute closure plan.

### Standard 3: Record

- Pilot ID and evidence manifest hash.
- Decision, rationale, conditions, action owners.
- Platform Owner account, signature reference, UTC timestamp.

---

## Implementation Plan

### Day 1 — Evidence

- [ ] Complete checklist.
- [ ] Link evidence and hashes.

### Day 2 — Decision

- [ ] Hold owner review.
- [ ] Sign decision.
- [ ] Open follow-up actions or closure.

---

## Cancellation Criteria

- No decision is valid if the evidence manifest is incomplete or unsigned.
