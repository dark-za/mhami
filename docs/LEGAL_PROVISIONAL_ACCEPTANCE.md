# Provisional Legal Documentation Acceptance

**Status:** Platform-owner provisional acceptance for internal preparation.

**Recorded:** 2026-08-30

## Decision

The current legal-document workspace is accepted as a provisional operational
baseline for internal development, staging, demonstrations without personal
data, and release-preparation work. This decision acknowledges that the
documents exist, are versioned, and are connected to the platform acceptance
model.

## Boundaries

This is not legal advice, legal counsel approval, a binding customer agreement,
or a finding of regulatory compliance. The documents under `docs/legal/` remain
placeholders until qualified counsel replaces and approves their text.

Until that review is recorded, the following remain blocked:

- production promotion involving real customer or employee personal data;
- external AI transfers containing personal data;
- real-user pilot operation; and
- representation that Mhami is legally or PDPL compliant.

## Required Before External Launch

1. Obtain qualified legal review for the terms, privacy notice, processor
   terms, AI transfer notice, employee privacy acknowledgement, retention and
   deletion policy, support access authorization, ROPA, DPIA, and breach
   response plan.
2. Replace each placeholder with approved text and record the effective
   version in its change log and the `LegalDocument` registry.
3. Record the owner and legal sign-offs in auditable release evidence.

This record does not override the legal and pilot gate in
`docs/PUBLIC_RELEASE_PLAN.md` or the dependency rule in `docs/legal/README.md`.
