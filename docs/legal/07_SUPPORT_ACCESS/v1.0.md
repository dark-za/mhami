## Product Inputs

- The company owner may grant and revoke support access at any time
  through `POST /api/v1/auth/company/support` and `DELETE
  /api/v1/auth/company/support`.
- Support access is **per named individual**, not per team. The
  grant is recorded in `SupportAuthorization` with a reason and an
  expiry.
- Every support action is **tenant-scoped** and audited. The audit
  event `SUPPORT_ACCESS_USED` records the support user, the company,
  the reason, and the expiry.
- Export and high-impact actions require the **current authorisation
  scope**; an expired or revoked grant fails closed.
- Platform support users authenticate with MFA and operate under the
  same audit and integrity guarantees as any other user.
