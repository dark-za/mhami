## Product Inputs

- **Active tenants.** Operational data is retained for the lifetime of
  the active contract plus the current export window. Audit history is
  retained per the audit policy in
  `docs/SECURITY_AND_DATA_BASELINE.md`.
- **Suspended or cancelled tenants.** Owners have **90 days of
  read-only export access** to the company data through the
  `exports` module. After the window:
  - Primary data is hard-deleted.
  - Backups expire through the documented backup cycle (no separate
    retention of identifiable data in backups).
  - Anonymised operational history may be retained where lawful
    audit obligations apply (e.g. financial records).
- **Evidence media.** Raw evidence is deleted when the task
  completes its retention clock. Blurred derivatives follow the same
  clock unless the company elects to keep a longer, documented
  evidence window.
- **Soft vs hard delete.** The lifecycle statuses
  `READ_ONLY → PENDING_DELETION → DELETED` are managed by
  `apps.tenancy.services.process_lifecycle_expirations` (daily
  sweep) and audited.
- **Backup data.** Backup contents are encrypted and removed through
  the documented backup-expiry process; backup restoration is treated
  as a controlled operation, not a retention bypass.
