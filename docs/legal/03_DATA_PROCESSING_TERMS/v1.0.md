## Product Inputs

- **Roles.** The tenant company is the controller; the platform is the
  processor. Sub-processors (hosting, backup, optional AI, email) are
  listed in `apps/compliance/manifest.py` once approved.
- **Authorised support access.** A named platform user may act on
  behalf of the company only when an active, non-expired
  `SupportAuthorization` exists for that company. Every support action
  is tenant-scoped, audit-logged, and individually attributable.
- **Data regions.** Stored in the documented production region. Any
  cross-border transfer (e.g. external AI) is permitted only after
  explicit owner acceptance (`LegalDocumentType.AI_TRANSFER`).
- **Retention.** Active tenants retain data under the
  `06_RETENTION_DELETION` policy. Suspended or cancelled tenants have
  90 days of read-only export access, then deletion. Backups expire
  through the documented backup cycle.
- **Deletion.** Primary data is hard-deleted at the end of the
  retention period. Backups are re-encrypted and overwritten on their
  cycle. Audit history is retained per the audit policy.
- **Sub-processor changes.** Material changes (new sub-processor, new
  region, new data category) require an updated DSR/Release
  notification and a re-acceptance of these terms.
