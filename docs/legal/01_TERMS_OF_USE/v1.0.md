## Product Inputs

- Company self-registration with a permanent company code chosen by the
  owner, a 30-day trial, manual extension, suspension, a 90-day
  read-only export window, and deletion.
- Company responsibility for its operational standards, branch setup,
  employee onboarding, and selection of any external AI provider.
- Versioned owner acceptance is required before continued
  administration after a material update.
- The platform provides infrastructure, audit, support authorization,
  and review tooling; it is not a substitute for the company's own
  employment, training, or compliance obligations.

## Acceptance Tracking

- Each acceptance of this document by an owner is recorded in
  `LegalAcceptance` (tenant-scoped, append-only, with
  `document_type="terms"` and the version string).
- When a new effective version is published, the owner is required to
  re-accept before continued administration of the company.
- Employee acknowledgement of the privacy notice is recorded
  separately under `LegalDocumentType.EMPLOYEE_PRIVACY`.

## Review and Change Log

Material changes must be recorded in `CHANGELOG.md` of this directory
with a date, author, summary, and approval reference. Annual review is
mandatory or sooner if there is a material product or regulatory change.
