## Product Inputs

- **Owner acceptance is required** before any external AI transfer is
  permitted for a tenant. The acceptance is recorded in
  `LegalAcceptance` with `document_type="ai_transfer"`.
- **Only the permitted data set is sent**: task criteria, opaque
  reference media identifiers, and blurred evidence derivatives. Raw
  evidence and employee PII are not sent to the AI provider.
- **Provider selection is company-controlled.** Endpoint, model, and
  credentials are configured by the owner under
  `apps/ai_gateway`/`apps/connector_control`. The platform never
  injects a default provider for production.
- **AI is a verification aid.** It does not certify regulatory
  compliance, and AI failure must not mark unsafe evidence as
  successful.
- **Revocation.** The owner can revoke the AI acceptance at any time;
  in-flight analyses are cancelled and no new requests are sent until
  the acceptance is restored.

## Acceptance Tracking

- Recorded in `LegalAcceptance` with
  `document_type="ai_transfer"`. The
  `apps/ai_gateway/services.py:is_external_ai_authorized_for_company`
  helper consults this record.
