## Product Inputs

- The acknowledgement is recorded at first use, before any task or
  evidence submission is permitted.
- The notice explains:
  - What task evidence is collected, by whom it can be reviewed, and
    for how long.
  - That evidence images may be blurred when faces are detected and
    that the original is held under stricter access controls.
  - That branch-scoped access is the default; only authorised monitors
    and the owner can see branch data outside the employee's branch.
  - That the company may add operational monitoring, training, and
    disciplinary procedures on top of the platform; the platform does
    not perform those.
  - That re-acknowledgement is required on a material update.
- This acknowledgement does not replace any company employment or
  privacy obligations.
