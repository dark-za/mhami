## Product Inputs

- The tenant company is the **data controller**. The platform is the
  **data processor** acting on the controller's documented instructions.
- Personal data categories processed: account data (login id, display
  name, contact channel), employee data (job role, branch membership,
  shifts), task data (assignments, evidence, notes, decisions), audit
  data (who did what, when, with what authorisation), and support
  access data (granted scope, reason, expiry).
- Evidence images are private. Face-blur derivatives are produced
  before the original is released for review when a face is detected
  by the approved server-side detector. The retention of raw vs.
  blurred media is described in `06_RETENTION_DELETION`.
- Employees acknowledge this notice on first use. Owners re-acknowledge
  on material updates.
- International customer scope and the final data region remain
  inventory-dependent and are not yet fixed.
